Thank you for visiting [kikoruiz.es](/) (hereinafter, "the Site"). Here I explain what personal data I process, what for, who I share it with and what rights you have.

## Data controller

The controller is me, Kiko Ruiz. My identification details are in the [legal notice](/legal-notice) and you can write to me at any time at hola@kikoruiz.es.

## What data I process and what for

- **Browsing:** when you visit the Site, technical data such as your IP address, browser type, operating system and how you interact with the pages are processed. I use them to make the Site work, to measure its performance and use, and to improve it. Legal basis: my legitimate interest in maintaining the Site and, for Google's analytics cookies, your consent.
- **Contact:** if you write to me, I process your name, your email and the content of the message in order to reply. Legal basis: your consent and the application of pre-contractual measures at your request.
- **Purchases:** if you buy, I process your name, your email, your billing address and, for prints, your shipping address, as well as the products and the amount. I use them to handle the payment, deliver your downloads or ship your prints, deal with possible returns and meet my tax and commercial obligations. Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract and compliance with legal obligations.
- **Purchase emails:** I email you the link to your downloads and, where applicable, messages about your order. Legal basis: performance of the contract.

I do not make automated decisions about you or build profiles with legal effects.

## Who I share your data with

I do not sell your data. To provide the service I share it with providers that process it on my behalf or on their own:

- **Stripe:** processes payments. I neither see nor store your card details.
- **Resend:** sends the purchase emails.
- **Cloudflare:** hosts the download files.
- **Vercel:** hosts the Site and measures its visits and performance without using cookies.
- **OpenStreetMap:** provides the maps shown on the Site. When you open a map, your browser requests it directly from its servers, which receive your IP address and the technical data of the request.
- **Google:** provides the Google Analytics 4 service, only if you accept analytics cookies.
- **Courier service:** receives your name and address when you buy prints, to deliver them to you.
- **Tax advisor:** has access to the data needed to meet my tax obligations.

I may also disclose data to the authorities when the law requires it.

## International transfers

Some of these providers, such as Stripe, Vercel, Google, Resend or Cloudflare, may process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States. They do so with the safeguards provided for by the rules, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## How long I keep your data

- Purchase data: for the periods required by tax and commercial rules.
- Contact messages: for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and, afterwards, for the limitation periods of possible liabilities.
- Analytics data: according to the retention periods set in Google Analytics.

After that time, I delete or anonymise them.

## Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability by writing to me at hola@kikoruiz.es and stating the right you want to exercise. If you have given your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting what has already been processed. If you think I have not processed your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the [Spanish Data Protection Agency](https://www.aepd.es).
